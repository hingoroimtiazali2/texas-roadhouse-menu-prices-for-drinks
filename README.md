# Texas Roadhouse Menu Prices for Drinks — A Simple Guide

Choosing a drink at Texas Roadhouse can shape the whole meal, whether you are settling in for a steak dinner or sharing appetizers with friends. This independent guide explains the main kinds of drinks you are likely to see, how to think about value, and what to ask your server before you order, so you can pick something that fits your taste and your budget.

## What to Expect from the Drinks Menu

The drinks list is usually organized into simple groups. Non-alcoholic choices often include soft drinks, iced tea, lemonade, coffee, and water, while the alcoholic side often features beer, wine, and mixed drinks. Some locations also offer frozen drinks and house specialties that change with the season or the local menu. Because selections can vary from one restaurant to another, it helps to read the menu at your table and check with your server about what is available that day.

## Non-Alcoholic Drinks Worth Considering

For many guests, a familiar soft drink or a glass of iced tea is the easiest choice with a hearty meal. Lemonade can feel refreshing on a warm day, and coffee can be a calm way to finish dinner. If you prefer something lighter, water is a straightforward option. When you are dining with children, it can help to ask about kid-friendly choices and refill policies before ordering, since practices can differ by location.

## Beer, Wine, and Mixed Drinks

Adults who drink alcohol often start by deciding between beer, wine, and a mixed drink. Beer pairs naturally with grilled food and casual appetizers, while wine can suit a slower dinner. Mixed drinks, including margaritas and other cocktails, are often chosen for flavor, garnish, and presentation as much as for the drink itself. If you are unsure, ask your server which options are popular, whether a drink is served frozen or on the rocks, and whether a smaller size is available. Always drink responsibly, and arrange safe transport if you plan to drink.

## How to Think About Drink Prices

Drink prices can vary by location, size, and type, so compare the options on the menu in front of you. A basic soft drink and a specialty cocktail are different kinds of choices, and the gap between them can be wide. A simple drink may be enough with a filling main course, while a specialty drink can be part of the occasion itself. If price matters, check the menu carefully, ask about sizes, and confirm before you order a second round.
